# Al-Manzumah al-Bayquniyyah (المنظومة البيقونية)
### Author: Umar ibn Muhammad al-Bayquni (d. circa 1080 AH)
*The Universal Classical Didactic Poem on Hadith Nomenclature (34 Poetic Verses with English Commentary)*

---

### Verses 1–5: Introduction & The Sahih (Authentic)
1. **أَبْدَأُ بِالْحَمْدِ مُصَلِّياً عَلَى ... مُحَمَّدٍ خَيْرِ نَبِيٍّ أُرْسِلاَ**  
   *I begin with praise, sending blessings upon Muhammad, the finest prophet ever sent.*

2. **وَذِي مِنْ أَقْسَامِ الْحَدِيثِ عِدَّهْ ... وَكُلُّ وَاحِدٍ أَتَى وَحَدَّهْ**  
   *And these are the categories of Hadith, numbered; and each one is introduced alongside its definition.*

3. **أَوَّلُهَا الصَّحِيحُ وَهْوَ مَا اتَّصَلْ ... إِسْنَادُهُ وَلَمْ يَشِذَّ أَوْ يُعَلْ**  
   *The first of them is the Sahih (Sound/Authentic), which is that whose chain is continuous, and is neither anomalous (shadhdh) nor deficient ('illat).*

4. **يَرْوِيهِ عَدْلٌ ضَابِطٌ عَنْ مِثْلِهِ ... مُعْتَمَدٌ فِي ضَبْطِهِ وَنَقْلِهِ**  
   *Narrated by an upright, precise transmitter from the like of him, relied upon in his precision and transmission.*

5. **وَالْحَسَنُ الْمَعْرُوفُ طُرُقاً وَغَدَتْ ... رِجَالُهُ لاَ كَالصَّحِيحِ اشْتَهَرَتْ**  
   *And the Hasan (Good) is that whose paths of transmission are known, but whose narrators do not attain the famous precision of the Sahih.*

---

### Verses 6–10: Weak Hadith, Marfu', Mawquf, and Maqtu'
6. **وَكُلُّ مَا عَنْ رُتْبَةِ الْحُسْنِ قَصُرْ ... فَهْوَ الضَّعِيفُ وَهْوَ أَقْسَاماً كَثُرْ**  
   *And all that falls short of the rank of Hasan is Da'if (Weak), and its sub-categories are numerous.*

7. **وَمَا أُضِيفَ لِلنَّبِي الْمَرْفُوعُ ... وَمَا لِتَابِعٍ هُوَ الْمَقْطُوعُ**  
   *Whatever is attributed to the Prophet is Marfu' (Elevated); and whatever is attributed to a Successor is Maqtu' (Severed).*

8. **وَالْمَوْقُوفُ مَا لِلصَّاحِبِ أُنِيمْ ... مِنْ غَيْرِ أَنْ يَرْفَعَهُ تَقِيمْ**  
   *And the Mawquf (Halted) is that which is ascribed to a Companion without elevating it.*

9. **وَالْمُرْسَلُ مِنْهُ الصَّحَابِيُّ سَقَطْ ... وَقُلْ غَرِيبٌ مَا رَوَى رَاوٍ فَقَطْ**  
   *And the Mursal is that from which the Companion has dropped; and call Gharib (Scarce) that which only a single narrator transmits.*

10. **وَكُلُّ مَا لَمْ يَتَّصِلْ بِحَالِ ... إِسْنَادُهُ مُنْقَطِعُ الأَوْصَالِ**  
    *And every chain that is not continuous under any condition is Munqati' (Broken in links).*

---

### Verses 11–15: Mu'dal, Mudallas, and Shadhdh
11. **وَالْمُعْضَلُ السَّاقِطُ مِنْهُ اثْنَانِ ... وَمَا أَتَى مُدَلَّساً نَوْعَانِ**  
    *The Mu'dal (Perplexing) is that from which two consecutive narrators have dropped; and that which comes Mudallas (Concealed) is of two types.*

12. **الأَوَّلُ الإِسْقَاطُ لِلشَّيْخِ وَأَنْ ... يَنْقُلَ عَمَّنْ فَوْقَهُ بِعَنْ وَأَنْ**  
    *The first is dropping one's immediate shaykh and transmitting from the one above him using 'an (from) or 'anna (that).*

13. **وَالثَّانِ لاَ يُسْقِطُهُ لَكِنْ يَصِفْ ... أَوْصَافَهُ بِمَا بِهِ لاَ يَنْعَرِفْ**  
    *The second does not drop him, but describes his attributes with that by which he is not commonly recognized.*

14. **وَمَا يُخَالِفْ ثِقَةٌ بِهِ الْمَلاَ ... فَالشَّاذُّ وَالْمَقْلُوبُ قِسْمَانِ تَلاَ**  
    *And that in which a trustworthy narrator contradicts the collective group of reliable narrators is the Shadhdh (Anomalous); and the Maqlub (Inverted) follows as two types.*

15. **إِبْدَالُ رَاوٍ مَا بِرَاوٍ قِسْمُ ... وَقَلْبُ إِسْنَادٍ لِمَتْنٍ قِسْمُ**  
    *Substituting one narrator for another is one type; and switching a chain to a different text is another type.*

---

### Verses 16–20: Mutawatir, Ahad, and Mawdu'
16. **وَالْفَرْدُ مَا قَيَّدْتَهُ بِثِقَهِ ... أَوْ جَمْعٍ أَوْ قَصْرٍ عَلَى رِوَايَهْ**  
    *And the Fard (Solitary) is that which is restricted to a single trustworthy narrator, a specific region, or a confined transmission.*

17. **وَمَا بِعِلَّةٍ غُمُوضٍ أَوْ خَفَا ... مُعَلَّلٌ عِنْدَهُمُ قَدْ عُرِفَا**  
    *And that which possesses an obscurity or hidden defect is Mu'allal (Defective) as recognized among the masters of the craft.*

18. **وَذُو اخْتِلاَفِ سَنَدٍ أَوْ مَتْنِ ... مُضْطَرِبٌ عِنْدَ أُهَيْلِ الْفَنِّ**  
    *And that which has conflicting discrepancies in chain or text is Mudtarib (Confused/Shaky) among the scholars of the discipline.*

19. **وَالْمُدْرَجَاتُ فِي الْحَدِيثِ مَا أَتَتْ ... مِنْ بَعْضِ أَلْفَاظِ الرُّوَاةِ اتَّصَلَتْ**  
    *And Mudrajat (Interpolated additions) in Hadith are words from certain narrators that become connected to the text without clear distinction.*

20. **وَالْكَذِبُ الْمُخْتَلَقُ الْمَصْنُوعُ ... عَلَى النَّبِي فَذَلِكَ الْمَوْضُوعُ**  
    *And the fabricated, forged lie invented against the Prophet ﷺ is the Mawdu' (Fabricated).*

---

### Conclusion (Verses 30–34)
30. **وَقَدْ أَتَتْ كَالْجَوْهَرِ الْمَكْنُونِ ... سَمَّيْتُهَا مَنْظُومَةَ الْبَيْقُونِي**  
    *And it has arrived like a hidden jewel; I have named it the Bayquni Poem.*

31. **فَوْقَ الثَّلاَثِينَ بِأَرْبَعٍ أَتَتْ ... أَقْسَامُهَا تَمَّتْ بِخَيْرٍ خُتِمَتْ**  
    *It has arrived in four-and-thirty verses; its categories are completed, sealed with goodness.*
