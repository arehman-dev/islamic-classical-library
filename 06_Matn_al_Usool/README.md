## Overview & Scholarly Importance
This section contains the classical primers (*Al-Mutun al-Asliyyah*) studied universally across Islamic seminaries to master the methodologies (*Usūl*) of four foundational disciplines:
1. **Usool al-Tafsir** (Methodology of Quranic Exegesis)
2. **Usool al-Hadith** (Hadith Nomenclature & Science of Transmission)
3. **Usool al-Fiqh** (Principles & Linguistic Rules of Jurisprudence)
4. **Usool al-Aqeedah** (The Foundations of Orthodox Sunni Creed)
