# Al-Aqeedah al-Wasitiyyah (العقيدة الواسطية)
### By Shaykh al-Islam Taqi al-Din Ahmad ibn Taymiyyah (661–728 AH)
*A Definitive Exposition of the Creed of the Saved Sect (Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah)*

---

### Introduction
All praise is due to Allah Who sent His Messenger with guidance and the religion of truth, that He may make it prevail over all religions; and sufficient is Allah as a witness. I bear witness that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah alone, without partner; and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and His Messenger, may Allah bless him, his family, and his companions with abundant peace.

This is the creed of the Saved Sect (*al-Firqah al-Najiyah*), which shall remain victorious until the Day of Resurrection: **Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah**.

It consists of belief in:
1. Allah
2. His Angels
3. His Books
4. His Messengers
5. The Resurrection after death
6. The Divine Decree (*al-Qadar*), both the good and evil of it.

---

### Core Principle Concerning the Divine Names and Attributes
From belief in Allah is to believe in what He has described Himself with in His Book (the Qur'an), and what His Messenger Muhammad ﷺ has described Him with, without:
- **Tahrif** (distortion or alteration of wording/meaning)
- **Ta'til** (divestment or negation of the attributes)
- **Takyif** (inquiring into or assigning 'how' or modality)
- **Tamthil** (comparing or anthropomorphizing Him with creation)

Rather, they believe that:
> *"There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing."* (Surah ash-Shura, 42:11)

They do not negate from Him that which He has described Himself with, nor do they distort the words from their proper contexts, nor do they deviate concerning His names and verses, nor do they liken His attributes to the attributes of His creation. For there is none comparable to Him, none equal to Him, none like Him, and He is not to be compared to His creation, Exalted is He.

---

### Affirmation of Transcendent Attributes
- **Knowledge**: He knows what has been, what will be, and what could never be how it would be if it were.
- **Power and Will**: Whatever Allah wills comes to pass, and whatever He does not will never comes to pass.
- **Hearing and Seeing**: He hears all sounds and sees all sights, whether subtle or manifest.
- **Speech**: The Qur'an is the uncreated Speech of Allah, originating from Him and returning to Him.
- **Loftiness and Ascendance (*Al-'Uluww wal-Istiwa'*)**: Allah is above the heavens, ascended over His Throne (*istiwa'an yaleequ bi-jalalih*), separate from His creation, and His knowledge encompasses all things.
- **The Beatific Vision (*Al-Ru'yah*)**: The believers will see their Lord with their eyes in the Hereafter, as clearly as they see the full moon on a cloudless night.

---

### The Position on Faith (Iman) and Sins
- Faith (*Iman*) consists of statement and deed: statement of the heart and tongue, and deeds of the heart, tongue, and limbs.
- Faith increases through obedience and decreases through disobedience.
- The people of the Sunnah do not declare anyone of the Qiblah to be an unbeliever on account of general sins or major sins, unlike the Khawarij.
- They love all the Companions of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, refrain from disputes that arose among them, and acknowledge the superior rank of Abu Bakr, 'Umar, 'Uthman, and 'Ali (may Allah be pleased with them all).
