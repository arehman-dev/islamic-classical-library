# The Three Fundamental Principles (Al-Usul al-Thalathah)
### By Shaykh Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab (1115–1206 AH)

---

### Introduction: Four Essential Matters
Know, may Allah have mercy on you, that it is obligatory upon us to learn four matters:
1. **Knowledge** (*Al-'Ilm*): which is the knowledge of Allah, knowledge of His Prophet, and knowledge of the religion of Islam with proofs.
2. **Action upon it** (*Al-'Amal bihi*): putting this knowledge into practice.
3. **Calling to it** (*Al-Da'wah ilayh*): inviting others to this truth.
4. **Patience upon harm encountered therein** (*Al-Sabr 'ala al-adha fihi*).

The proof is the statement of Allah, the Exalted:
> *"By time! Indeed, mankind is in loss; except for those who have believed and done righteous deeds and advised each other to truth and advised each other to patience."* (Surah al-'Asr, 103:1–3)

---

### The Three Fundamentals Every Muslim Must Know
When it is asked of you: *"What are the three fundamentals that every person is obligated to know?"*
Say:
1. **The servant's knowledge of their Lord** (*Ma'rifat al-Rabb*).
2. **The servant's knowledge of their Religion** (*Ma'rifat al-Din* with proofs).
3. **The servant's knowledge of their Prophet, Muhammad ﷺ** (*Ma'rifat al-Nabi*).
