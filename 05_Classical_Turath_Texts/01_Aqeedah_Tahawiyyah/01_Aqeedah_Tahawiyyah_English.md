# The Creed of Imam al-Tahawi (Al-Aqidah al-Tahawiyyah)
### By Imam Abu Ja'far Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Tahawi (239–321 AH)
*An Exposition of the Creed of the People of the Sunnah and the Community (Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah)*

---

### Introduction
The great scholar, Hujjat al-Islam Abu Ja'far al-Warraq al-Tahawi (may Allah have mercy on him) said in Egypt:

This is an exposition of the creed of Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah according to the doctrine of the jurists of the religion: Abu Hanifah al-Nu'man ibn Thabit al-Kufi, Abu Yusuf Ya'qub ibn Ibrahim al-Ansari, and Abu 'Abdullah Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Shaybani (may Allah be pleased with them all), and what they believed concerning the foundations of the faith and by which they worshiped the Lord of the worlds.

---

### 1. The Oneness of Allah (Tawheed)
1. We say concerning the divine unity of Allah—believing by Allah's divine assistance—that Allah is One, having no partner.
2. There is nothing like unto Him.
3. There is nothing that can overwhelm Him or render Him incapable.
4. There is no deity other than Him.
5. He is Ancient without beginning, Eternal without end.
6. He does not perish, nor does He cease to be.
7. Nothing happens except what He wills.
8. No imagination can reach Him, and no intellect can comprehend Him.
9. He does not resemble any created being.
10. He is Living and never dies; All-Sustaining and never sleeps.
11. He creates without needing to, and provides without incurring any hardship.
12. He causes death without fear, and resurrects without difficulty.
13. He has always existed with His attributes before His creation; nothing was added to His essence by their coming into existence that was not already His. Just as He was from eternity with His attributes, so shall He remain throughout eternity.
14. It was not after creating creation that He gained the name "the Creator", nor through fashioning beings that He gained the name "the Originator".
15. He possessed the meaning of Lordship when there was no object of Lordship, and the meaning of Creator when there was no created thing.
16. Just as He is the Reviver of the dead after having revived them, He was entitled to this name even before reviving them; similarly, He was entitled to the name Creator before creating them.
17. This is because He has power over all things, and all things are dependent upon Him. Every matter is easy for Him, and He has no need of anything: *"There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing"* (Surah ash-Shura, 42:11).

---

### 2. Divine Decree and Predestination (Al-Qadar)
18. He created creation with His knowledge.
19. He appointed for them their destined measures.
20. He set for them their appointed life spans.
21. Nothing was hidden from Him before He created them, and He knew all that they would do before creating them.
22. He commanded them to obey Him and forbade them from disobeying Him.
23. Everything occurs by His decree and will. His will is accomplished; creatures have no will except what He wills for them. What He wills for them comes to pass, and what He does not will never comes to pass.
24. He guides whomever He wills, protects them, and keeps them safe out of His pure grace; and He leads astray whomever He wills, abases them, and tests them out of His absolute justice.
25. All of them move within His will between His grace and His justice.
26. He is exalted above partners and opposites.
27. None can repel His decree, none can delay His judgement, and none can overpower His command.
28. We believe in all of this, and we have certitude that all of it proceeds from Him.

---

### 3. Prophethood of Muhammad ﷺ
29. And we believe that Muhammad ﷺ is His chosen servant, His select prophet, and His approved messenger.
30. He is the Seal of the Prophets, the Imam of the pious, the leader of the messengers, and the beloved of the Lord of the worlds.
31. Every claim to prophethood after him is falsehood and delusion.
32. He was sent to all of the Jinn and mankind with truth and guidance, with light and illumination.

---

### 4. The Holy Qur'an
33. The Qur'an is the Speech of Allah. It originated from Him as words without modality (kayfiyyah). He sent it down upon His messenger as revelation.
34. The believers affirm it as truth in reality. They have certainty that it is the Speech of Allah, Exalted is He, in reality.
35. It is not created like the speech of human beings. Whosoever hears it and claims it is human speech has committed disbelief. Allah has censured and threatened such a person with the fire of Hell: *"I will cast him into Saqar"* (Surah al-Muddaththir, 74:26). Since Allah threatened with Saqar the one who said *"This is nothing but the words of a mortal"*, we know with certainty that it is the Speech of the Creator of mortals and does not resemble the speech of mankind.
36. Whosoever describes Allah with any human characteristic has committed disbelief. Whoever perceives this takes admonition, refrains from the statements of disbelievers, and knows that Allah, with His attributes, is not like humans.

---

### 5. The Beatific Vision of Allah (Al-Ru'yah)
37. The Beatific Vision of Allah by the people of Paradise is a reality, without encompassing and without modality, as stated in the Book of our Lord: *"Faces that Day shall be radiant, looking at their Lord"* (Surah al-Qiyamah, 75:22–23).
38. Its explanation is as Allah willed and knew. Everything that has reached us in authentic traditions from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ concerning this is as he said, and its meaning is as he intended. We do not interpret this through our personal opinions, nor do we fantasize according to our whims. No one is safe in their religion except one who surrenders to Allah, Exalted is He, and to His Messenger ﷺ, and refers what is unclear to him back to the One Who knows it.

---

### 6. The Ascending Journey (Al-Mi'raj) and the Basin (Al-Hawd)
39. The Ascension (Al-Mi'raj) is true. The Prophet ﷺ was taken on the night journey in his bodily person while awake to the heavens, and then to whatever high stations Allah willed for him. Allah honored him with what He willed and revealed to him what He revealed: *"His heart did not lie about what he saw"* (Surah an-Najm, 53:11). May Allah bless him and grant him peace in the Hereafter and in the first life.
40. The Basin (Al-Hawd) with which Allah has honored him as a relief for his Ummah is true.
41. The Intercession (Al-Shafa'ah) which has been stored up for them is true, as reported in the traditions.

---

### 7. The Covenant, Divine Knowledge, and the Pen
42. The Covenant (Al-Mithaq) that Allah took from Adam and his offspring is true.
43. Allah, Exalted is He, has known from eternity the exact number of those who will enter Paradise and those who will enter the Fire; that number shall not be increased nor decreased.
44. The same applies to their actions: He knew what they would perform. Everyone is facilitated toward that for which they were created. Deeds are judged by their conclusions. The fortunate one is fortunate by the decree of Allah, and the wretched one is wretched by the decree of Allah.
45. The basis of the Divine Decree (Al-Qadar) is Allah's secret in His creation. No angel brought near nor any prophet sent has been granted knowledge of it. Delving deeply into it leads to abandonment, denial, and deviation. So beware, take every precaution against investigating it through contemplation, thought, or whisperings.
46. We believe in the Tablet (Al-Lawh) and the Pen (Al-Qalam) and all that has been inscribed upon it. If all of creation gathered together to prevent something that Allah wrote would occur, they could not prevent it; and if they gathered to bring about what He wrote would not occur, they could not do so. The Pen has dried with all that is to exist until the Day of Resurrection.

---

### 8. The Throne (Al-'Arsh) and Footstool (Al-Kursi)
47. The Throne (Al-'Arsh) and the Footstool (Al-Kursi) are realities.
48. He has no need of the Throne or anything below it.
49. He encompasses everything and is above it, and has rendered His creation incapable of full comprehension.

---

### 9. Angels, Heavenly Books, and Messengers
50. We say with faith, affirmation, and submission that Allah took Ibrahim as an intimate friend (khalil) and spoke directly to Musa (kalim).
51. We believe in the angels, the prophets, and the revealed scriptures sent down to the messengers. We bear witness that they were all upon the manifest truth.
52. We refer to the people who pray toward our Qiblah as Muslims and believers, as long as they acknowledge what the Prophet ﷺ brought and affirm all that he stated and conveyed.

---

### 10. Sins, Faith, and Excommunication (Takfeer)
53. We do not delve into disputes regarding the essence of Allah, nor do we dispute in the religion of Allah.
54. We do not argue about the Qur'an; rather we testify that it is the Speech of the Lord of the worlds, brought down by the Trustworthy Spirit upon the heart of the Master of Messengers ﷺ.
55. We do not declare any of the people of the Qiblah to be disbelievers on account of a sin they commit, as long as they do not deem it lawful (halal).
56. We do not say that sin causes no harm to the one who commits it when accompanied by faith.
57. We hope for the righteous among the believers that Allah will forgive them and admit them to Paradise by His mercy, but we do not grant them absolute certitude of Paradise, nor do we testify that they are among its dwellers. We seek forgiveness for their evil-doers, fear for them, but do not make them despair.
58. Feeling completely secure from Allah's reckoning and completely despairing of His mercy both expel a person from the religion of Islam. The path of truth for the people of the Qiblah lies precisely between the two.

---

### 11. The Nature of Iman (Faith)
59. Faith consists of affirmation by the tongue and belief with the heart.
60. All that Allah has revealed in the Qur'an, and all that is authentic from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ regarding the sacred law, is true.
61. Faith is one in its essence, and its adherents are equal in its foundation. Differentiation between them occurs through God-consciousness (taqwa), piety, overcoming desires, and adhering to what is best.
62. All believers are the allies (awliya') of the All-Merciful, and the most noble among them in the sight of Allah are the most obedient and followers of the Qur'an.

---

### 12. The Companions of the Prophet ﷺ (Al-Sahabah)
63. We love the companions of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. We do not go to excess in the love of any one of them, nor do we disown any of them.
64. We detest anyone who hates them or mentions them with evil; we mention them only with good. Loving them is religion, faith, and excellence; hating them is disbelief, hypocrisy, and transgression.
65. We affirm the Caliphate after the Messenger of Allah ﷺ:
    - First for **Abu Bakr al-Siddiq** (may Allah be pleased with him), affirming his precedence over the entire Ummah;
    - Then for **'Umar ibn al-Khattab** (may Allah be pleased with him);
    - Then for **'Uthman ibn 'Affan** (may Allah be pleased with him);
    - Then for **'Ali ibn Abi Talib** (may Allah be pleased with him).
    These are the Rightly-Guided Caliphs (Al-Khulafa' al-Rashidun) and righteous leaders.
66. We testify that the Ten whom the Messenger of Allah ﷺ named and promised Paradise are in Paradise, exactly as he bore witness. His word is truth. They are: Abu Bakr, 'Umar, 'Uthman, 'Ali, Talhah, al-Zubayr, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, Sa'id ibn Zayd, 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf, and Abu 'Ubaydah ibn al-Jarrah—the trustee of this Ummah (may Allah be pleased with them all).
67. Whoever speaks well of the companions of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, his pure wives free of every blemish, and his noble progeny cleansed of every defilement, is free from hypocrisy.

---

### 13. The Scholars and the Saints (Awliya')
68. The early scholars from the righteous forebears (Salaf) and those who succeeded them—the people of goodness, virtue, jurisprudence, and discernment—are to be mentioned only with excellence. Whoever speaks ill of them is not upon the straight path.
69. We do not elevate any saint (wali) above any of the prophets (peace be upon them); rather we say: A single prophet is greater than all the saints combined.
70. We believe in the miracles (karamat) that have been authentically transmitted regarding them.

---

### 14. Signs of the Hour and the Afterlife
71. We believe in the signs of the Day of Judgment: the emergence of the Anti-Christ (al-Dajjal), the descent of 'Isa ibn Maryam (peace be upon him) from heaven, and the rising of the sun from the west.
72. We do not believe in soothsayers, diviners, or anyone who claims anything that contradicts the Book, the Sunnah, and the consensus of the Ummah.
73. We hold that unity upon truth is correct, and separation is deviation and torment.
74. The religion of Allah in the heavens and on earth is one: the religion of **Islam**. Allah, Exalted is He, says: *"Indeed, the religion in the sight of Allah is Islam"* (Surah Al 'Imran, 3:19) and *"And I have approved for you Islam as your religion"* (Surah al-Ma'idah, 5:3).
75. Islam lies between excess and neglect, between anthropomorphism and denial of divine attributes, between fatalism and the negation of divine decree, and between false security and despair.

---

### Conclusion
This is our religion and our creed, inwardly and outwardly. We absolve ourselves before Allah of everyone who opposes what we have mentioned and clarified. We ask Allah, Exalted is He, to establish us upon faith, conclude our lives with it, protect us from corrupt whims, divergent doctrines, and reprehensible sects... We ask Him for protection from all deviation, and by Him is all success achieved.
