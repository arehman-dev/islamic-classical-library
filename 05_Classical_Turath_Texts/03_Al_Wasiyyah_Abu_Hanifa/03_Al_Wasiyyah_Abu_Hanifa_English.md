# Al-Wasiyyah (The Testament)
### By Imam al-A'zam Abu Hanifah al-Nu'man (80–150 AH)
*A Concise Exposition of Orthodox Faith and Moral Advice Dictated to His Companions*

---

### Introduction
Imam Abu Hanifah (may Allah be pleased with him) said:
> *"Know, my brothers and companions, that the doctrine of the People of the Sunnah and the Community (Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah) rests upon twelve foundational principles. Whosoever adheres to them with steadfastness, believes in them with conviction, and lives by them, shall not be among the innovators or deviants."*

---

### The Twelve Articles of Faith

#### 1. The Nature of Iman (Faith)
Faith (*iman*) consists of confession with the tongue, belief with the mind, and certainty with the heart. Confession alone does not suffice without internal belief, for had confession alone been faith, all the hypocrites would have been believers. Nor does knowledge alone suffice without verbal confession, for had knowledge alone been faith, all the people of the scripture who recognized the Prophet would have been believers:
> *"And they recognized him as they recognize their own sons."* (Surah al-Baqarah, 2:146)
Faith neither increases nor decreases in its essential nature; rather, it increases and decreases in terms of certainty, certitude, and righteous actions.

#### 2. The Divine Decree (Al-Qadar)
We confess that Allah decrees and measures both good and evil. Whosoever claims that Allah does not decree evil, or that evil occurs without His will, has attributed weakness to Allah and stripped Him of sovereign Lordship. Everything that exists in the heavens and on earth occurs through His knowledge, His decree, His divine will, and His tablet.

#### 3. Human Actions and Divine Creation
We confess that actions are of three categories:
1. **Obligatory** (*fard*): performed by Allah's command, will, pleasure, decree, and assistance.
2. **Virtuous** (*fadilah*): performed not by strict command, but with His will, pleasure, decree, and love.
3. **Disobedient** (*ma'siyah*): committed not by His command, but within His knowledge, His decree, His predestination, and His abandonment—without His love or pleasure.

#### 4. The Istiwa' (Ascendance) of Allah over the Throne
We confess that Allah ascended over the Throne (*al-Istiwa' 'ala al-'Arsh*) as an established reality without Him having any bodily need for it or settling upon it. He is the Preserver of the Throne and of everything besides the Throne without any need. Had He been in need of it, He would have been incapable of originating the universe and sustaining it like created beings.

#### 5. The Quran as Uncreated Divine Speech
We confess that the Holy Qur'an is the uncreated Speech of Allah, His inspiration, and His revelation. It is not He, nor is it other than He; rather, it is His attribute in reality. It is written in books, recited by tongues, and heard by ears, yet the ink, paper, writing, and reciting are created, while the Speech of Allah itself is uncreated.

#### 6. The Superiority of the Rightly-Guided Caliphs
We confess that the most virtuous of this Ummah after our Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is:
1. **Abu Bakr al-Siddiq**
2. Then **'Umar ibn al-Khattab**
3. Then **'Uthman ibn 'Affan**
4. Then **'Ali ibn Abi Talib**
May Allah be pleased with all of them. We love all of them, pray for their forgiveness, and hold our tongues from anything that transpired between them.

#### 7. Sin and the Believer
We confess that a believer does not become an unbeliever on account of committing a sin, even if it is a major sin, as long as they do not believe it to be lawful. The sinner remains a believer whose final destination rests with the will of Allah: if He wills, He punishes them with justice in the Fire, and if He wills, He pardons them through His grace and the intercession of the Prophet ﷺ.

#### 8. The Intercession (Al-Shafa'ah)
We confess that the Intercession of our Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is a true reality for all believers who committed major sins from among his Ummah, as authentically reported in the traditions.

#### 9. Realities of the Grave and Afterlife
- The questioning by **Munkar and Nakir** in the grave is true.
- Paradise and Hell are already created and will never perish.
- The **Mizan** (Scale) on the Day of Judgment is true.
- The reading of the record of deeds is true.
- The Beatific Vision of Allah in the Hereafter is true for the dwellers of Paradise without modality, direction, or comparison.

---

### Moral and Spiritual Exhortation
Imam Abu Hanifah concluded his testament by advising:
> *"Fear Allah with genuine fear. Uphold the prayer at its appointed times. Cleanse your hearts of envy, rancor, and arrogance. Deal with people with noble character and kindness. Fulfill your covenants, speak truth even against yourselves, and be mindful of your final return to Allah."*
