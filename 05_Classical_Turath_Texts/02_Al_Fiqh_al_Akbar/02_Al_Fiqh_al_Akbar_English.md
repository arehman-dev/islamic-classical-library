# Al-Fiqh al-Akbar (The Greater Understanding)
### By Imam al-A'zam Abu Hanifah al-Nu'man ibn Thabit (80–150 AH)
*The Earliest Extant Systematic Primer of Islamic Orthodoxy*

---

### Article 1: The Foundation of Faith (Tawheed)
The foundation of faith in the divine oneness of Allah and that which is incumbent upon a person to believe is to say:
> *"I believe in Allah, His Angels, His Books, His Messengers, the Resurrection after death, the Divine Decree—both the good and bad of it being from Allah, the Most High—the Reckoning, the Balance, Paradise, and the Hellfire; and that all of these are true realities."*

---

### Article 2: The Transcendent Oneness of Allah
Allah, the Exalted, is One; not in terms of numerical count, but in the sense that He has no partner or equal:
> *"Say: He is Allah, the One. Allah, the Absolute Refuge. He neither begets nor is born, nor is there to Him any equivalent."* (Surah al-Ikhlas, 112:1–4)

He does not resemble any of His creation, nor does any of His creation resemble Him.
He has always existed, and will always exist, with His names and attributes—both those of His essence (*dhatiyyah*) and those of His actions (*fi'liyyah*).

---

### Article 3: The Pre-Eternal Attributes of Allah
The attributes of His Essence include:
- **Life** (*al-Hayah*)
- **Power** (*al-Qudrah*)
- **Knowledge** (*al-'Ilm*)
- **Speech** (*al-Kalam*)
- **Hearing** (*al-Sam'*)
- **Sight** (*al-Basar*)
- **Will** (*al-Iradah*)

The attributes of His Action include:
- **Creating** (*al-Takhliq*)
- **Sustaining** (*al-Tarziq*)
- **Originating** (*al-Insha'*)
- **Fashioning** (*al-Ibda'*)
- **Making** (*al-Sun'*)
and all other attributes of divine action.

He has always existed with all of His attributes and names; no new attribute or name has ever come into being for Him. He was knowing before creation with His eternal knowledge, capable with His eternal power, speaking with His eternal speech, creating with His eternal power to create, and doing with His eternal action. The Creator is not emergent, and His action is eternal without beginning.

---

### Article 4: The Uncreated Speech of Allah (The Qur'an)
The Holy Qur'an is the Speech of Allah, the Exalted, inscribed in the copies of the text (*masahif*), preserved in the hearts, recited upon the tongues, and revealed unto the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
- Our uttering of the Qur'an is created, our writing of it is created, and our reciting of it is created;
- But the Qur'an itself—the divine, uncreated Speech of Allah—is uncreated.
- Whatever Allah mentions in the Qur'an concerning Moses and other prophets (peace be upon them), and concerning Pharaoh and Satan, all of it is the Speech of Allah informing us about them. The Speech of Allah is uncreated, whereas the speech of Moses and other creatures is created.

---

### Article 5: Transcendence Above Creaturely Characteristics
Allah is a reality (*shay'*) unlike any other reality. When we say He is a "thing" (*shay'*), we mean He is affirmed in existence without physical body (*jism*), without substance (*jawhar*), without accidental property (*'arad*), without spatial boundary (*hadd*), without opposite (*didd*), without peer (*nidd*), and without equal (*mithl*).

He possesses a Hand (*Yad*), a Face (*Wajh*), and a Self (*Nafs*), as He has mentioned in the Qur'an. Whatever Allah has mentioned in the Qur'an regarding the Face, the Hand, and the Self belongs to Him as attributes without modality (*bila kayf*). It cannot be said that His Hand is His power or His favor, because that leads to the nullification of the attribute, which is the path of the deviant sects. Rather, His Hand is His attribute without modality, and His Anger and Pleasure are two of His divine attributes without modality.

---

### Article 6: Creation of Human Acts and Human Acquisition (Kasb)
Allah created all creatures free from both disbelief and belief. He then addressed them, commanded them, and prohibited them.
- Whoever disbelieved did so by their own choice, denial, and rejection of the truth through Allah's abandonment of them;
- Whoever believed did so by their own choice, affirmation, and verification through Allah's divine facilitation and grace.
All actions of human beings—both movement and rest—are in reality acquired (*kasb*) by them, while Allah, the Exalted, is their sole Creator. All of them occur by His will, His knowledge, His decree, and His predestination.

---

### Article 7: Infallibility of the Prophets ('Ismah)
All the Prophets (peace and blessings be upon them) are free from minor sins, major sins, disbelief, and repugnant acts. However, lapses and slips may occur from them, which Allah corrects immediately.

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is His beloved, His servant, His messenger, His prophet, His chosen one, and His pure friend. He never worshiped idols, nor did he ever associate partners with Allah for even a single blink of an eye, and he never committed a major or minor sin.

---

### Article 8: The Companions of the Prophet ﷺ
The most virtuous of all people after the Messenger of Allah ﷺ are:
1. **Abu Bakr al-Siddiq** (may Allah be pleased with him)
2. **'Umar ibn al-Khattab** (may Allah be pleased with him)
3. **'Uthman ibn 'Affan** (may Allah be pleased with him)
4. **'Ali ibn Abi Talib** (may Allah be pleased with him)

We love all the companions of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, we refrain from speaking of them except with praise and honor, and we mention none of them with anything but goodness.

---

### Article 9: Sins and the Preservation of Faith
We do not declare any Muslim to be an unbeliever on account of any sin they commit—even if it is a major sin—as long as they do not consider it lawful (*halal*).
The name of faith (*iman*) is not stripped from them; rather, we still refer to them as a believer in truth. It is possible for a person to be a believer who is a transgressor (*fasiq*), without being a disbeliever (*kafir*).

Deeds are distinct from faith, and faith is distinct from deeds. A person may sometimes be excused from performance of an act of worship (such as a menstruating woman excused from prayer), but they are never excused from faith.

---

### Article 10: Realities of the Afterlife
- The questioning in the grave by **Munkar and Nakir** is a true reality.
- The return of the soul to the servant in the grave is true.
- The punishment and bliss of the grave are true realities for all who deserve them.
- The **Resurrection** (*al-Ba'th*), the **Balance** (*al-Mizan*), the **Reckoning** (*al-Hisab*), the **Basin** (*al-Hawd*), and the **Bridge** (*al-Sirat*) are true realities.
- **Paradise** (*al-Jannah*) and the **Hellfire** (*al-Nar*) are created realities that exist at this present moment; they shall never perish nor pass away throughout eternity.
- The believers will see Allah, the Exalted, while they are in Paradise with their physical eyes, without comparison, without modality, without direction, and without any distance between Him and His creation.

---

### Conclusion
This is the pristine creed upon which the Companions, the Successors, and the Imams of Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah have traversed. Whosoever adheres to it is upon the manifest truth and the straight path.
